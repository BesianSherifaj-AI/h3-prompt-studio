# Sketch and movement tools

Open the creative tools panel from the editor's toolbar. Work stays in the panel while it is open; export or add a reference before closing it.

**Sketch reference:** choose Pen, Arrow or Rectangle, then drag on the canvas. Pick a color and line width. Undo restores the previous drawing, including a cleared canvas. Export PNG saves a 736 × 416 image; Add PNG to references puts it in your local library. Choose its role and review its description before enabling it for generation.

**Movement layout:** select a subject, shot duration, paths and pace. It starts with the selected timeline row's duration, which may be shorter than the whole video's four-second minimum. Drag the filled start markers and outlined end markers. Top-down and Isometric 3D show the same positions. Use the selected marker's X/Z fields for precision, or focus a marker and use arrow keys in 0.1-unit increments; Shift moves one unit. Static camera copies its start position to its end. Reset layout restores the initial positions.

Review the editable movement instructions, then use the button to add them to the selected shot. Changing the layout refreshes the draft instructions. The duration describes that planned move and does not retime your project. You can export a labeled guide PNG or add it to your references.

The planner is a spatial guide on a relative 10 × 10 floor, with an eye-level camera aimed at the subject. It does not simulate motion or export a movie, and it does not create H3 keyframe controls. Labels and arrows remain in exported guides; choose their reference role deliberately.
